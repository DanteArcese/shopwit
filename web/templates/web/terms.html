What UnitPricer is

UnitPricer is a free, open-source price-per-unit comparison for groceries and household goods.

Prices are not offers

Prices are shown as of the time displayed and may change or be wrong. Check the retailer before you buy. Prices in the "Seen in stores" section are observations recorded by people in stores, not live prices. We make no warranty about any price, availability or product detail.
