Disclosure

Affiliate links

We may earn a commission when you buy through links on this site. UnitPricer participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program, Walmart's affiliate program through Impact, and Sovrn Commerce. Links to those retailers may carry our affiliate tracking, and are marked as sponsored.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Ranking is commission-blind

Products are ranked only by unit price (ties are broken by price, then product title, then retailer). Whether a retailer pays a commission never affects ranking or which products are shown.

Price freshness

Every price shows when it was last seen. Prices and availability are accurate as of that date and time and are subject to change. Only recently seen prices are listed.

Links

Links go directly to the retailer or the affiliate network's own tracking address; there is no UnitPricer redirect. We count outbound clicks anonymously, recording only the product offer, the retailer and the time. No personal data is stored.

Data sources
