Privacy

UnitPricer is a free, open-source price comparison site run by Dante Arcese. This page says what it stores about you, who handles it and how to have it deleted. There are no ads, no tracking pixels and no advertising or cross-site tracking. We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and doesn't store IP addresses or build profiles of visitors.

What we collect

  • Browsing (home, categories, search, product pages): nothing is stored about you and no cookies are set. Search terms are used to find products; phrases we do not yet cover may be counted anonymously (the phrase and a tally, with no link to you), and each phrase is deleted 30 days after it was last searched for.
  • Store announcements ("Notify me about new stores"): your email address and the times you signed up, confirmed and were last sent an email. Nothing else; there is one list.
  • Saved settings: the units, stores, ZIP code and distance you choose in Settings. They are stored on our server in your session record (linked to your browser by a session cookie, expiring 60 days after you last save your settings or lists) or, if you have an account, with your account. The ZIP code is used only to show in-store prices from nearby stores and is never logged. Clear it in Settings to remove it.
  • Accounts and lists, described below.
  • Feedback, described below.

Accounts and lists

  • Accounts are optional; lists work without one, saved in your session. If you create an account (by asking for a sign-in link), we store your email address, a password hash only if you set a password (never the password itself), when the account was created and last signed in, your lists and your saved settings (units, stores, ZIP code and distance). No names, IP addresses, user agents or tracking ids.
  • Sign-in links: we store your email address with a one-way hash of the link (never the link itself). A link works once and expires after 15 minutes; the record is deleted within two days after it expires, or at once if you delete your account. Sign-in emails are sent through Resend.
  • To limit abuse, we count sign-in attempts per email address and per IP address under salted one-way hashes in a temporary cache. The counts expire within an hour and are deleted within a day after that. Emails, links and IP addresses are never logged.
  • To delete your account, sign in, open Account and use Delete account. That deletes your email address, password hash, lists and saved settings at once. Or email hello@unitpricer.com and we will delete it for you.

Feedback

  • The feedback form stores what you send: the type, your message, a store name and website for store requests, the product you reported (if any) and, only if you give it, an email address for a reply. We do not store your IP address; it is used only, in hashed form, to limit how many messages one visitor can send per hour.
  • Your reply email is deleted when we close or reject your item, and in any case 90 days after you sent it.
  • Nothing is published without our review. Approved bug reports, ideas and other feedback may be posted publicly as issues on our GitHub repository (your message text, with email addresses and links made inert), and approved store requests as a public request count for that store. Your email address is never posted. Reports of a wrong price or size stay private.

Why

To confirm your signup and tell you when a new store goes live, to sign you in and keep your lists and settings, to show prices near you, to keep the site safe from abuse, and to read and act on feedback you send (including replying if you asked us to). Nothing else.

Who handles it

  • DigitalOcean (New York): hosting.
  • Cloudflare: network, security and rate limiting for the whole site, access control for the staging site, and Cloudflare Web Analytics (aggregate page views, referrers, browser and country; no cookies and no stored IP addresses).
  • Resend: delivery of confirmation, sign-in and announcement emails.
  • Cloudflare R2: off-site copies of database backups.
  • GitHub: public issues for approved feedback (never your email address).
  • Sentry: error reports, only if switched on. Each report has the error type and message, the code lines where it happened (no variable values), breadcrumbs (the most recent server log lines and database query templates before the error, scrubbed the same way), the page address without anything after a ?, your browser type, the time, and the software versions the site runs on. It never has cookies, form data, search words, your IP address, any sign-in or signup link token, any email address or ZIP code (those are replaced before sending).
  • Backups: nightly dumps kept on our server (newest 7), DigitalOcean's daily server backups (kept 7 days) and off-site copies in Cloudflare R2 (kept 30 days).

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else. If you follow a link to a store, that store's own privacy policy applies from there.

Cookies

  • Browsing sets no cookies from us.
  • __Host-sessionid: starts only when you save settings, use a list, sign in or send feedback. It keeps your lists, settings and sign-in. It expires 60 days after last use.
  • __Host-csrftoken: set only when you open a page with a form that needs protecting from forgery (settings, lists, sign-in, feedback, confirm and unsubscribe pages). The "Notify me about new stores" form on the home page has no token and sets no cookie.
  • Cloudflare may set its own security cookies. CF_Authorization applies only to the staging site, not to unitpricer.com.

Logs

Server logs hold no IP addresses, email addresses, search terms or ZIP codes. Cloudflare, which sits in front of the site, sees IP addresses as part of delivering and protecting it, under its own privacy policy.

How long

  • Unconfirmed signups are deleted after 7 days.
  • Confirmed signups are kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Feedback reply emails: deleted when the item is closed or rejected, or after 90 days. The rest of a feedback item is kept until we delete it.
  • Accounts, with their lists and settings, are kept until you delete them. Session records expire 60 days after last use.
  • Search phrases we do not yet cover: deleted 30 days after last seen.
  • Backups age out as described above.

Your choices

Use the unsubscribe link in any email we send, which deletes your address. Delete your account from the Account page. Clear your ZIP code in Settings. Or email hello@unitpricer.com to see or delete your data. Questions to hello@unitpricer.com or the maintainer at dante@unitpricer.com.
